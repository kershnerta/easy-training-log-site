Easy Training Log is a local-first fitness log developed by Travis Kershner. This policy explains how version 1.0 handles information.

No account required

You can use the app without creating an account or providing contact information to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The app stores workout templates, active workout drafts, completed workouts and sets, cardio activities, schedules, preferences, goals, achievements, optional Fitness Profile information, Fitness Test results, and notes in local app storage. Optional Fitness Profile information may include date of birth, scoring category, height, and weight when you choose to provide it for Fitness Test scoring.

Information collected by the developer

Easy Training Log does not transmit your training or profile information to the developer. Version 1.0 contains no developer analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting, or behavioral-tracking service. The developer does not sell user information.

Exports, sharing, and imports

CSV and JSON exports and workout-share files are created only when you request them. A file leaves the app only when you select a destination through the operating system share sheet. The selected destination handles that file under its own terms. Workout imports are files you choose and are processed locally. JSON export is not a complete restore backup.

Device and platform services

Your operating system, App Store, TestFlight, device-backup provider, or a destination selected in the share sheet may process information under its own policies. Depending on your settings, local app data may be included in an encrypted device or iCloud backup. Easy Training Log does not automatically upload data to a developer-operated cloud service and does not control platform backup settings.

Permissions

Version 1.0 does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, HealthKit, motion data, or tracking. Optional links to this policy, support information, and official Fitness Test source material require an internet connection only when you choose to open them.

Your controls and retention

Information remains in the app container until you edit or delete it, erase all app data, or remove the app, subject to device backups you control. Settings provides controls to delete workout history, delete Fitness Test history, clear the optional Fitness Profile, or erase all app data.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children because it transmits no user information to the developer. Fitness Test scoring tables may have minimum ages defined by their official sources.

Changes and contact

If the app later adds accounts, synchronization, analytics, advertising, payments, health-platform access, or another network service, this policy and the App Store privacy declaration will be updated before release. For privacy questions or support, use the public contact method on the Support page.
