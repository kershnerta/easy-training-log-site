Privacy Support

Spend less time logging

Build workouts, record each set independently, and pick up from your previous session. Log Run, Walk, Bike, Row, Swim, StairMaster, and Elliptical activities live or after you finish.

Remember what worked

Previous-workout memory and explainable progression guidance help you decide what to do next without flattening mixed sets.

See your progress

Review training history, exercise-appropriate trends, weekly goals, achievements, Fitness Tests, and general challenges.

Private by design

No account, advertising, analytics, or behavioral tracking. Your training data stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.
