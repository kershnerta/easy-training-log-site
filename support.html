If the app will not open

Restart your iPhone and try again. If the problem continues, note your iPhone model, iOS version, and the app version/build shown in Settings → About when available, then report the problem below.

Workout and activity logging

Use My Workouts for saved workout plans. Use Quick Activity for Run, Walk, Bike, Row, Swim, StairMaster, or Elliptical entries. You can track live or log a completed activity manually.

Restore or resume an active workout

An unfinished workout or genuinely started activity is saved locally. Return to the active-session banner to resume it, or discard it if you no longer need the draft.

Exports

Settings provides CSV and JSON export through the iOS share sheet. Confirm the chosen destination has available storage. JSON export is not currently a complete restore backup.

Fitness Tests

Some official scores require Fitness Profile details such as age, scoring category, height, or weight. Source and effective-date information appears with supported tests.

Deleting data

Settings includes separate history-deletion controls and Erase All App Data. Deletion is permanent unless your platform restores an earlier device backup.

Report a problem

Open a public support issue and include the app version/build, iOS version, what happened, and steps to reproduce.

Do not post your date of birth, passwords, private health details, workout exports, or other sensitive personal information.
